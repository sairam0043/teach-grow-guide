Tutor payments — how it works

How money reaches a tutor: what we collect from them, how their monthly total is calculated, and who is responsible at each step.

Status — planned, not yet built

This describes the system as designed, not as it works today. Right now tutor payments are calculated in the admin dashboard and transferred manually. Do not quote timelines or statuses in this document to a tutor yet. This page will be updated when each part goes live.

01 Overview

Students pay upfront when they book. That money is held by the company, and the tutor is paid their share once a month for the classes they actually delivered.

The system calculates each tutor's monthly total automatically and prepares a payment batch. A person reviews and approves that batch before any money moves. Nothing is transferred without a human releasing it.

The approval step is deliberate. If a calculation is ever wrong, it produces a wrong number on a screen rather than a wrong transfer to every tutor at once.

02 How a tutor gets paid

End to end, from signup to money in the account. Steps marked in amber need a person.

1

Tutor submits payment detailsTutor

A one-time form in profile settings: bank account, PAN, and the supporting documents listed in section 3.

2

Bank account verified automaticallySystem

A ₹1 test transfer confirms the account exists and returns the name the bank holds. This catches wrong account numbers before any real payment.

3

Details approvedOperations

Someone checks the documents against the submitted details and approves. A tutor cannot be paid until this happens.

4

Classes are delivered and marked completeTutor / System

Only completed classes count toward payment. Cancelled and no-show classes do not.

5

Month-end totals calculatedSystem

The system sums each tutor's completed classes for the month, applies the commission split, and creates a payment record awaiting approval.

6

Batch reviewed and releasedFinance

The full batch is reviewed line by line and released. This is the only point at which money actually moves.

7

Transfers sent and confirmedSystem

Payments go to each tutor's bank account. Status is tracked until the bank confirms, and failures are flagged for follow-up.

04 How the amount is worked out

Three rules, applied in order.

1 · Only delivered classes count

A tutor is paid per class actually completed, not per class booked. For a monthly pack, the amount the student paid is divided across the classes in that pack, and the tutor is paid for the ones delivered. Six of twelve classes delivered means half the pack value.

2 · The commission split is applied

The company retains a percentage and the tutor receives the rest. The current system uses 90 / 10; moving to 70 / 30 is under discussion but not yet decided — see section 9.

3 · The rate in force at the time is used

If a tutor changes their hourly rate mid-month, classes are paid at whatever rate applied on the day they were taught. Rate changes are never applied retroactively.

Because the commission is taken from what the student actually paid, discounts reduce both sides proportionally. On the three-day monthly pack — which already gives students 30% off — this compounds noticeably. How to handle that is open; see section 9.

08 Common questions

When will tutors be paid?

Once a month, for classes completed in the previous month. The exact day is not yet fixed — see section 9.

What if a tutor's payment fails?

The payment is marked Failed with a reason from the bank, and Operations follows up. Usually it is an incorrect account number or a closed account. The tutor corrects their details and the payment is retried in the next run — the amount is not lost.

What happens if a tutor has not submitted their details?

Their earnings accumulate and are held. Nothing is forfeited. They are paid once their details are submitted and approved, in the next monthly run.

What if a class was cancelled or the tutor did not attend?

Only completed classes are paid. Cancelled classes are excluded from the total. For monthly packs the value is spread across the classes in the pack, so an undelivered class simply is not counted.

Can a tutor change their bank account?

Yes, through profile settings. Any change re-runs the ₹1 verification and needs approval again before the next payment. The tutor is emailed whenever their details change — this is a security measure, since changing a bank account is a common fraud route.

Is tax deducted from a tutor's payment?

Not decided yet, and it needs input from our accountant. This is why PAN is mandatory — without one on file, tax is deducted at a substantially higher rate.

Why does a tutor need to give a PAN at all?

It is a legal requirement for making payments of this kind in India, and it is how the tutor's identity is confirmed against their bank account.
